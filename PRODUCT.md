# PRODUCT.md — Harsh Upadhyay, portfolio

Written in the shape Impeccable (`/impeccable init`) expects: durable product truth, no visual direction.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Harsh Upadhyay, a self-taught full-stack web developer and BCA student in
Faridabad, Haryana. The site has one job: convince a visitor — a recruiter, a freelance client, or
another developer — that a person with no formal CS degree yet still ships real, working products.
Then get them to open a project or send an email.

Success = the visitor opens at least one project, or starts a conversation.

## Audience

1. **Recruiters / hiring managers** at small product companies and agencies. Skimming, 20–40 seconds,
   often on a phone. They want role, stack, proof, contact.
2. **Freelance clients** with a project in mind. They want to see the quality of shipped work and a
   fast, low-friction way to reach out.
3. **Peer developers** arriving from GitHub or X. They judge on craft and honesty, not on titles.

## Positioning

Early-career, evidence-first. The differentiator is not a decade of experience — it is that the work
exists and runs. Three shipped applications, deployed and clickable, written by someone in his first
semester of a BCA. The honesty is the brand: no inflated job titles, no fake metrics, no "10x".

Anti-positioning: not a design agency, not a senior consultant, not a generic "passionate developer
who loves clean code" template.

## Operating Context

- Static site, no backend, no build step required. Must run by opening `index.html` and deploy
  unchanged to Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages.
- Content is English. Audience reads English; the design should not require Hindi.
- Visitors arrive from a link in a résumé, a DM, or a GitHub profile. Mobile share is likely > 50%.
- Assets are local (self-hosted fonts, WebP screenshots) so the page survives a sandboxed preview,
  a slow Indian mobile connection, and a CDN-less deploy.

## Voice

First person, plain, specific, a little dry. Short sentences. Concrete nouns over adjectives.
No exclamation marks. Confident because the work is there, not because the copy says so.

Do: "Three apps, deployed and clickable. I built all three while learning."
Don't: "A passionate developer on a journey to craft delightful digital experiences."

## Constraints

- Nothing may be invented: no client names, no fake testimonials, no fabricated user counts or
  revenue. If a number is not in Evidence, it does not ship.
- Project links point at the live deployments the visitor can actually click.
- Must hold up on a 360px viewport with one hand.

## Non-goals

Redesigning the projects themselves. A blog. A CMS. Dark mode as a feature (the site commits to one
warm-light world instead of shipping a second, weaker one).
